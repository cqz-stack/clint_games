Battling Monsters

Survivor-style sandbox — collect gems, level up, shop with gold, and fight through 50 stages with giant bosses every 10.

Desktop game (Python + Tkinter) — play on your computer, not in the browser.

Controls

  • WASD / arrows — move (weapons fire automatically)
  • Pick gems for XP · visit the shop for upgrades

How to play

cd battling_monsters
python3 monsters.py

Or open it from the What If? hub:

python3 games_hub.py
