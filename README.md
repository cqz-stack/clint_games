# clint_games

A few games made by clint with AI. Started when he is 9. 
They are all two player games.
Clint also got help from his dad named Michael. 
This AI is called Cursor.

## Play (What If?)

**What If?** is the hub title screen. It lists every game; click one to open that game’s own title screen.

```bash
/opt/anaconda3/bin/python3.12 games_hub.py
```

- Catalog: [`games_registry.py`](games_registry.py) — add a `GameEntry` when you upload a new game  
- Launch wiring: [`games_hub.py`](games_hub.py) — add a function in `LAUNCHERS`

## Periodic Wall (desktop)

Python + Tkinter — lives in [`periodic_wall/`](periodic_wall/). Full game: `python3 periodic.py` from that folder (or open it from the hub). Legacy: `reference/periodic.py`.

## Global Windstorm

Dodge flying debris across 5 storms — [`global_windstorm/`](global_windstorm/). Also listed in the What If? hub.

## Expanding Earth

Race several screens; dodge trunks, waves, and rocks — [`expanding_earth/`](expanding_earth/). Also in the What If? hub.

## Geometry Dash

Hard long auto-runner — [`geometry_dash/`](geometry_dash/). Spikes, pads, orbs. Also in the What If? hub.

## Battling Monsters

Survivor.io-style infinite sandbox — gems, level-ups, 10 stages × 3 bosses — [`battling_monsters/`](battling_monsters/). Also in the What If? hub.

## Raising a Colony

Play as an ant or termite queen — lay eggs, grow your nest, ally, and fight rivals — [`raising_a_colony/`](raising_a_colony/). Also in the What If? hub.
