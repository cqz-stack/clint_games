Global Windstorm

Duck and quad-jump through 5 storms of flying debris. Red markers warn before every throw.

Desktop game (Python + Tkinter) — play on your computer, not in the browser.

Controls

  • A / D or arrows — move
  • W / ↑ / Space — jump (up to 4 jumps)
  • S / ↓ — duck

How to play

cd global_windstorm
python3 windstorm.py

Or open it from the What If? hub:

python3 games_hub.py
