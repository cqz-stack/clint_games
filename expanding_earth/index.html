Expanding Earth

Race several screens to the beacon while the crust heaves. Dodge falling trunks, giant waves, and rocks. 5 lives · 8 levels (you get slower each level).

Desktop game (Python + Tkinter) — play on your computer, not in the browser.

Controls

  • A / D or arrows — move
  • W / ↑ / Space — jump (including over waves)

How to play

cd expanding_earth
python3 earth.py

Or open it from the What If? hub:

python3 games_hub.py
